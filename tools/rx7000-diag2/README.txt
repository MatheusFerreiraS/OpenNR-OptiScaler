OpenNR RX 7000 diagnostic kit 2

This tests a fix for the mochizuki NR picture that was wrong and flickered on your RX 7000 card.
It runs the released OpenNR 0.5.8 network and the fixed one on a test picture at the six sizes that
looked worst last time (960x540 up to 2560x1440), three times each, and compares every result with a
picture made on an RX 9070 XT. It does not install anything and does not touch your games or drivers.

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example your Desktop). Keep about 1 GB free there.
2. Close games and other heavy programs.
3. Double-click run-test.bat. If Windows says it protected your PC, click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. Wait. It takes about 10 to 15 minutes. It downloads the model files again (about 250 MB) from the
   same place the OpenNR installer uses. You can skip that download by copying dlssnr.bin and
   dlssnr-small.bin from the first kit's files\dlssnr-amd folder into this kit's files\dlssnr-amd folder.
5. When it says "Done", send us the one file it names:
   OpenNR-RX7000-diag2-result-<date>.zip (next to run-test.bat; the folder opens by itself).

If the window closes or the PC freezes, restart, then zip the "result" folder, send it and tell us
what happened.

You can delete the whole folder afterwards.
