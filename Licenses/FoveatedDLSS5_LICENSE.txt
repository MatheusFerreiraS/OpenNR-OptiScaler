FoveatedDLSS5
=============

Project:  FoveatedDLSS5 (a RenoDX DLSS 5 add-on fork)
Source:   https://github.com/maximedgoulet/FoveatedDLSS5

What is derived from it
-----------------------

The foveated working grid in OptiScaler/dlssnr/amd/Foveation.h: the per-axis density
profile (full density in a centre zone, a smoothstep fall-off, an edge density
beyond it), its integral G and the Newton inverse used in the fall-off band, the
nearest-depth and warped-displacement rules for the guides, and the 5% minimum
saving below which the grid stands down.
